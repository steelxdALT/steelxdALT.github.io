Accessibility

Accessible by design.

Some accessibility foundations are implemented, and the remaining work is being tracked here before launch claims are made.

  • Implemented foundationsSkip link, visible focus styles, display controls, reduced-motion handling, and mobile menu focus behavior are present.
  • Keyboard-only testVerify focus order, dropdown dismissal, dialog focus, and every actionable control without a mouse.
  • Zoom and large textTest at browser zoom and with the large-text setting to confirm content remains readable without overlap.
  • Screen readersTest with NVDA and VoiceOver before describing screen-reader support as complete.
  • Motion, contrast, and touchVerify reduced motion, contrast, mobile target sizing, captions, and spoken training instructions as those features are built.
